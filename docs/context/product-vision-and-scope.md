# Product Vision and Scope

## Purpose

**Marked by Francene** is a web reservation platform for a solo makeup artist studio. It replaces manual social-media DMs and spreadsheet scheduling with a structured booking funnel, automated slot availability calculation, downpayment receipt verification, portfolio showcase, and client appointment management.

## Business Objectives

- Eliminate double bookings through real-time availability computation and atomic slot holds.
- Standardize service pricing, extra-head counts, add-ons, and studio vs. on-location venue fees.
- Streamline manual downpayment confirmation without requiring live payment gateway fees at launch.
- Showcase curated bridal, event, and editorial looks alongside verified client testimonials.

## In-Scope MVP Capabilities

- **Public Discovery:** Landing experience, categorized makeup portfolio lookbook, service/package catalog, and verified client reviews.
- **Client Accounts:** Registration, email verification, sign-in/sign-out, password recovery, and profile management.
- **Availability & Booking:** Dynamic slot discovery in `Asia/Manila`, service and add-on selection, studio or on-location address entry, optional inspiration photos, and atomic slot holds.
- **Deposit Verification:** Client upload of bank/e-wallet downpayment proof and admin approval, re-upload request, or decline actions.
- **Self-Service Changes:** Client cancellation and rescheduling within configurable cutoff windows.
- **Notifications:** Queued lifecycle email updates and scheduled pre-appointment reminders.
- **Studio Admin Operations:** Service catalog CRUD, adjusting available weekly schedule and blackout management, booking verification queue, portfolio publishing, and review moderation.

## Explicit Non-Goals (Out of Scope for MVP)

- Automated online card or e-wallet checkout gateways (Stripe, PayMongo) and webhook reconciliation.
- SMS or push notification delivery.
- Native iOS or Android applications.
